Delete Your AnimePedia Account & Data

Last updated: 4 October 2026

AnimePedia lets you permanently delete your account and the data associated with it at any time. This applies whether you signed in with Google or are using the app as a guest.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open AnimePedia and sign in with Google if you haven't already.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This immediately and permanently deletes your Firebase Authentication account and your bookmarked-quotes backup from our Firestore database. It cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app or the device you used it on, email jdevsappstudio@gmail.com from the address associated with your Google account (or tell us the email you signed in with) and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm once it's done, typically within a few days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account — your Firebase Authentication identity (Google sign-in link, name, email, profile photo on file) is permanently removed.
  • Your cloud data — your bookmarked quotes backed up to Firestore are permanently removed.
  • Your local data — bookmarks, preferences, and theme choice stored on your device are removed when you uninstall the app, or immediately if you delete your account while signed in.

Anonymous, aggregated analytics/crash data already sent before deletion (via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) is not personally identifiable and is retained per Google's standard Firebase data retention policy — see our Privacy Policy for details.

Questions

Email jdevsappstudio@gmail.com with anything else about your data.
